## Business Environment

The retail environment requires management to balance several competing objectives:

- Revenue growth
- Customer acquisition and retention
- Product profitability
- Competitive pricing
- Discount management
- Regional expansion
- Channel performance
- Cost control

A high-revenue business is not necessarily a highly profitable business.

For example, a product may generate substantial revenue but have a relatively low gross margin because of high product costs or aggressive discounting.

Similarly, a customer segment may have a lower average margin but generate greater absolute gross profit because of higher transaction volumes.

Therefore, NexaMart requires analysis at multiple business levels rather than relying on a single company-wide KPI.

The project does not treat a single KPI as sufficient for evaluating business performance.
